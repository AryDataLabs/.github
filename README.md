## What is AryDataLab?

**AryDataLab** is an independent AI and data engineering lab focused on turning complex data and machine-learning problems into **production-ready systems**.

The lab operates across the full lifecycle:

> **Data → Modeling → AI Systems → MLOps → Production → Measurement**

Our work combines applied data science, machine learning engineering, cloud architecture, data engineering, and intelligent automation to build systems that can operate reliably beyond a notebook or proof of concept.

The organization is led directly by **Aryanto, M.Si**, a senior data scientist and AI/MLOps architect with **9+ years of production experience**, work across **10+ industries**, **28+ shipped ML/AI systems**, and experience mentoring **15+ data professionals**.

## Engineering Principles

### 01 — Production Over Prototype

A successful experiment is not the finish line.

AryDataLab emphasizes deployment, monitoring, testing, CI/CD, observability, and documentation so models can survive real operational environments.

### 02 — Measure What Matters

AI should connect to outcomes.

Projects are framed around measurable business or operational metrics such as:

- conversion and CTR improvement
- automation hours saved
- latency reduction
- revenue contribution
- operational efficiency
- model quality and stability

### 03 — Data Is Infrastructure

Reliable AI starts with reliable data.

We care about ingestion, transformation, data quality, feature pipelines, architecture, lineage, and the systems required to keep models healthy in production.

### 04 — Build for the Real World

Solutions should adapt to existing infrastructure instead of forcing organizations into unnecessary rewrites.

AryDataLab works across cloud-native, hybrid, and existing enterprise data environments.
